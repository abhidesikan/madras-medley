Namaste! I am Abhishek :)

I am from Chennai — Madras, as it was known when I grew up — and after a decade in the US, I have moved back home. I work as a Senior Software Engineer at Amazon. I have loved reading and writing since I was young, and this site is where I collate all of it in one place.

Why Madras Medley? Fondness for my city, and because I dabble in many things that interest me — so this site is a medley of my thoughts.

what you will find here:

  • book reviews — mostly Indian history, culture and civilization, and the occasional novel
  • a slow pilgrimage to the 108 Divya Desams — 41 of 108 so far
  • essays and reflections on work, family, genetics, mythology and more
  • travel writing, from temple towns to a decade in America
  • articles I have published elsewhere, gathered back home
