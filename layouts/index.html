Namaste! I am Abhishek :)

My name is Abhishek Desikan, and I am from Chennai, India. I work for Amazon as a Senior Software Engineer (full-stack).

I’ve loved reading and writing since I was young. Being a stickler for organization, I felt the need to collate all these writings in one spot, which gave birth to this website.

Why Madras Medley? I have a lot of fondness for my city Chennai, where I grew up, known as Madras back then. And I dabble in many things which interest me, and hence this site is a “medley” of my thoughts!

what you will find here:

  • book reviews — mostly Indian history, culture and civilization, and the occasional novel
  • a slow pilgrimage to the 108 Divya Desams — 41 of 108 so far
  • essays and reflections on work, family, genetics, mythology and more
  • travel writing, from temple towns to a decade in America
  • articles I have published elsewhere, gathered back home
